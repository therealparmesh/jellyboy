## Description

jellyboy is a small, independent client for personal Jellyfin media servers.

Connect to your server and keep movies, shows, music, and Live TV together in a focused library. jellyboy tries compatible playback on the device first and asks the server to convert media only when needed or when you choose a lower quality.

Features include:

- Movies, shows, episodes, music, and Live TV
- A library picker matching the signed-in Jellyfin user's named libraries
- Direct playback with AVPlayer and VLCKit
- Quality, speed, audio, and subtitle controls
- Authenticated artwork and thumbnails
- Downloads with selectable quality, audio, and subtitles
- AirPlay on compatible native streams
- Background audio and system media controls
- Light, dark, and system appearance modes
- Red and blue retro color palettes

A Jellyfin server and account are required for personal media. jellyboy does not provide, host, or sell media.

jellyboy is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or maintained by the Jellyfin project.

## Rights and naming review

- The listing may describe jellyboy as an independent client for Jellyfin servers. Do not use “Jellyfin” as the app name or imply official status.
- Jellyfin's published branding guidance discourages names in the `Jelly[word]` pattern. Confirm permission or select a distinct name before public distribution.
- Because “jellyboy” may also evoke Nintendo's Game Boy brand when paired with the interface, obtain appropriate trademark review before publishing.
- Do not mention Pokémon, Nintendo, Game Freak, Game Boy, or specific game titles in App Store metadata without documented permission.
- Confirm rights to the app name, icon, screenshots, and every other submitted asset before external TestFlight or App Store distribution. The bundled font and frame pixels are original jellyboy constructions, but that does not resolve separate naming or overall trade-dress concerns.
